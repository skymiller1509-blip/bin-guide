# Demo video script — Bin Guide (Arbiter Hacks V1)

- Target length: **about 2 minutes 30 seconds** (event needs 2–3 minutes, YouTube or Vimeo).
- Format: phone screen recording of the real app, read aloud in plain words. Everything shown is the app working — no slides, no mock-ups.
- The sentence a judge should repeat an hour later: **"You type what you are holding, and it tells you the right bin, in big letters, in seconds."**

## Hook options — pick the clearest, say it aloud, keep the best take

1. "Plastic bottle. Recycling. That took three seconds."
2. "A greasy pizza box in the recycling can spoil a whole bale of card. This tells you before you bin it."
3. "Which bin does it go in? Type it and find out."

## Shot-by-shot script

### 0:00–0:05 — Hook (sound off must still work)

- On screen, big text: **"WHICH BIN? TYPE IT. FIND OUT."**
- Already on screen behind the text: the app with `plastic bottle` typed in and the answer showing in big green letters: **RECYCLING**.
- Say: "Plastic bottle. Recycling. That took three seconds."

### 0:05–0:20 — Problem, one sentence, one named user

- On screen: the app home screen, empty search box.
- Say: "Ada is standing at the bins holding a greasy pizza box, and she has about five seconds to decide. Most people guess — and guessing wrong is worse than not recycling at all, because one greasy box can spoil a whole bale of card, and one battery in general waste can catch fire in a bin truck. This is Bin Guide, for Arbiter Hacks, theme track Rethinking Waste."

### 0:20–1:50 — The one live flow

Film the app on the phone, one item at a time. Type slowly so the camera can follow. Read each answer out loud.

- **Plastic bottle (0:20–0:45).** Type `plastic bottle`, tap Check the bin. The screen shows RECYCLING in big letters, the reason — "Empty plastic bottles melt down and become new bottles" — and the tip: "Squash it flat so the bin fits more." Say: "Plastic bottle: recycling. It tells me why, and gives me one tip."
- **Greasy pizza box (0:45–1:10).** Tap "Check another item", type `pizza box`, tap Check the bin. The screen shows COMPOST: "Grease soaks into the card, and recyclers reject greasy card." Say: "A greasy pizza box is not recycling — it is compost. This is the mistake the app exists to catch."
- **Battery (1:10–1:35).** Tap "Check another item", type `battery`, tap Check the bin. The screen shows HAZARDOUS: "Batteries can catch fire in bin trucks." Say: "A battery never goes in any home bin. It is hazardous — take it to a shop drop-off."
- **Browse (1:35–1:50).** Scroll to "Browse everything" and tap through the bin tabs. Say: "Or browse all 59 items by bin. Everything is on the phone — no account, no internet needed after the first load."

### 1:50–2:10 — How it works (30 seconds, plain words)

- On screen: the app, then a quick glance at `data.js` if it is open in a tab — optional, keep it to a few seconds.
- Say: "Underneath it is a built-in guide of 59 common household items, each with its bin, its reason, and one tip. You type words, it matches them — no model, no network, nothing leaves your phone. That is a choice: it is fast and private, and it never guesses."

### 2:10–2:30 — Honest limits and what's next

- Say: "Two honest limits. The app does not read photos — there is no image model in it, and it says so on the screen. A photo is only your own record; the answer comes from the words you type. And the rules are the common ones, not your council's — every answer says check your council. Next I would grow the list from the items people search for and do not find. Bin Guide — type it in, get the right bin in seconds."
- End screen, big text: **"TYPE IT. BIN IT RIGHT."**

## What NOT to say or show

- Do NOT tap the Photo button and imply the app reads the photo. If the photo button appears on screen, say the line above: the app does not read photos.
- Do not claim 59 items covers everything. An unknown item gets an honest "I do not know that one yet" — you may show that once instead of the browse section if you prefer, by typing a made-up item, but do not fake a bin answer for it.
- Do not claim the app was tested on a real phone browser if it was not — the matcher was tested in Node (25/25) and the pages served over HTTP. Film the real app and let the footage speak.
- Do not show any council's logo or claim this is any council's official list.

## Recording notes (phone)

- Android screen recorder: swipe down twice, tap Screen record, microphone ON, wait for the countdown.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb first, so no notification lands mid-take.
- Film in portrait — this is a phone app, and the judges watch on phones too. Keep the phone steady, brightness up.
- Open the app before you start recording (GitHub Pages link, or `index.html` in the browser). Close extra tabs.
- Rehearse the three items once before recording so the typing is smooth. If a take goes wrong, keep going — a small stumble is fine, a re-record is cheap.
- Add captions in CapCut (free) after recording — judges may watch with sound off.
- Record the hook (0:00–0:05) two or three times and keep the best take.
- Also save a separate 60-second insurance capture of the plastic bottle and battery checks, in case anything breaks on submission day.
